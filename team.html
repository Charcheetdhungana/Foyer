Team Foyer

The people behind Foyer

We built Foyer because organising an event is far more complicated than it should be. Our answer is a single platform where you can plan, manage and run every part of your event.

Charchit founded Foyer and leads its product vision, design and security. With a strong focus on user experience, Charchit makes sure Foyer is simple to use for organisers and attendees alike, while keeping every account and personal detail secure.

How we built Foyer

Four steps, repeated until it felt right.

01

Research

We studied more than 50 event platforms to see where organisers and attendees get stuck.

02

Design

We designed two separate experiences: one for businesses running events, one for people going to them.

03

Build

Event pages, tickets with QR codes, check-in and profiles — all sharing one record per attendee.

04

Test

We walked through every page as a first-time visitor on desktop and phone, and fixed what confused us.
