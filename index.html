For businesses

Every part of your event, managed in one place

Most small organisers juggle one tool for bookings, another for emails, a spreadsheet for the guest list and a printed sheet at the door. Details get copied by hand from one to the next, and that is where things go wrong — the same seat is sold twice, or guests turn up in the wrong room. Foyer puts it all in one place. Every guest has one record, from the moment they book to the moment they walk in.

Venue and catering built in Book the space and the food alongside your tickets. Catering is shown in the ticket price.
The exact room, on every ticket Attendees see the room, a map and directions — not just a street address.
One record per attendee From sign-up to check-in to the recording afterwards. No spreadsheets, no retyping.
Your first event is free No platform fee until your second event. Attendees never pay a booking fee.

What we offer

Everything an event needs, under one login.

01

Venues

Pick a venue from our list or type in your own, and tell attendees exactly which room to go to.

02

Catering

Add food and drink and set a price per head. It is shown in the ticket price, so there are no surprises.

03

Tickets

Attendees register and receive a digital ticket with a QR code straight away.

04

Check-in

Scan the QR code with your phone camera, or type the code. Duplicates are caught instantly.

05

Venue map

Every event page and ticket shows the room, a map of the venue and one-tap directions.

06

Recording

Add a recording link after the event and everyone who registered can watch it from their profile.
